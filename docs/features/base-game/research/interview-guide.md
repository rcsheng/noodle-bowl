# Alpha User Interview Guide — v0.1.0

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2026-05-02  
**Author:** rcsheng  
**Target:** Friends and early testers who have used the app for at least one session.

---

## Setup

- Duration: ~20 minutes
- Format: Casual conversation — not a survey. Let them talk, follow the thread.
- Record if possible (with permission). Otherwise take notes on surprises, not answers.
- Resist explaining or defending. Your job is to listen.

---

## Warm-Up (2 min)

1. How long have you been using the app? How often have you opened it?
2. Walk me through the last time you opened it. What did you do?

---

## First Impressions (3 min)

3. What was your first reaction when you saw the app?
4. Was anything confusing right away — anything you couldn't figure out without help?
5. Did the app explain itself well enough, or did you feel like you needed a tutorial?

---

## Games (8 min)

6. Which game did you play the most? Which did you avoid? Why?
7. For the games you played — did you feel like you understood the rules after the first round?
   - *(Follow up if no:)* Where did it lose you?
8. Was there a moment in any game that felt especially satisfying? What happened?
9. Was there a moment that felt unfair, frustrating, or just broken? What happened?
10. How long does a typical session last for you? Does that feel right, or does it feel too short / too long?

---

## Daily Habit (3 min)

11. Is there anything that made you want to come back the next day? What was it?
12. Is there anything that made you *not* want to come back? What was it?
13. What would have to change for you to open this app every morning?

---

## Social & Sharing (2 min)

14. Did you try sending a challenge or asking a friend for help? How did that go?
    - *(If no:)* What stopped you?
15. Would you share this app with someone else right now? Who, and why?

---

## Wrap-Up (2 min)

16. If you could add one thing to the app, what would it be?
17. If you could remove one thing, what would it be?
18. What's the one word you'd use to describe the app to a friend?

---

## Notes Template

Use this after each session:

```
Tester: [initials only]
Date:
Session length: 
Games played:

Most used game:
Least used game:
Biggest confusion:
Biggest delight:
Feature request mentioned:
Churn risk signals:
Surprise / unexpected finding:
```

---

## What to Look For

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| Tester explains a game rule incorrectly | Onboarding gap |
| Tester mentions checking the app unprompted | Habit forming |
| Tester asks "is there a way to…" for something that exists | Discoverability problem |
| Tester describes a rule as "unfair" | Scoring or pacing issue |
| Tester didn't know the challenge/help features exist | Social feature buried |
| Tester mentions a competitor app | Positioning signal |
