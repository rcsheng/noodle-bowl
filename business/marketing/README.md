# Marketing

This folder stores marketing assets generated with the Claude Cowork **marketing** skills.

## Skills that output here

| Skill | What it produces |
|---|---|
| `marketing:campaign-plan` | Campaign briefs, content calendars, channel strategy |
| `marketing:content-creation` / `marketing:draft-content` | Blog posts, social media, emails, landing pages |
| `marketing:email-sequence` | Multi-email nurture/onboarding sequences |
| `marketing:seo-audit` | Keyword research, on-page analysis, content gap reports |
| `marketing:competitive-brief` | Competitor positioning and messaging comparison |
| `marketing:brand-review` | Brand voice compliance checks with severity ratings |
| `marketing:performance-report` | Campaign performance summaries with next-step recs |

## Suggested subfolders

- `campaigns/` — campaign briefs and calendars by initiative
- `content/` — blog posts, social copy, email drafts
- `seo/` — keyword research and audit reports
- `competitive/` — competitor briefs and battlecards
- `reports/` — performance summaries

> Files in this folder are git-ignored. Keep originals backed up outside this repo.
