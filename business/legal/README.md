# Legal

This folder stores legal documents generated with the Claude Cowork **legal** skills.

## Skills that output here

| Skill | What it produces |
|---|---|
| `legal:triage-nda` | NDA triage classification reports |
| `legal:review-contract` | Contract redlines and negotiation strategy |
| `legal:compliance-check` | Compliance analysis for actions and features |
| `legal:legal-risk-assessment` | Risk severity/likelihood assessments |
| `legal:vendor-check` | Vendor agreement status and gap analysis |
| `legal:signature-request` | Pre-signature checklists and signing order configs |
| `legal:legal-response` | Templated responses to legal inquiries |
| `legal:meeting-briefing` | Legal meeting prep and action items |
| `legal:brief` | Daily legal briefings and topic research |

## Suggested subfolders

- `contracts/` — vendor, partner, and customer agreements
- `ndas/` — NDAs and triage reports
- `compliance/` — compliance checks per feature or initiative
- `risk/` — legal risk assessments

> Files in this folder are git-ignored. Keep originals backed up outside this repo.
